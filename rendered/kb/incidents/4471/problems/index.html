How each report was placed

  1. One report, once. The export pages overlap, so some reports come twice. They are counted by id (uniqueById).
  2. The tenant, not the browser. Each report is placed by its tenant's locale and booking form, the desk's own view (causes.ts, locales.ts). Through booking-v3's form it is hint; anything else from a day-first tenant is parser.
  3. The desk's own reading on top. A report in desk-notes.json → oneDayMove describes a date that slid by a day, not a day and a month that swapped: that is shift. As the desk files it, it stays counted under its tenant's cause too, so the rows add up to more than the ticket.

Why the subject rule merged them

Decision 0001 merges on subject alone, and every one of these reports says some version of "wrong day". The subject cannot tell a month-first parser from a form hint from a time shift; only the tenant, the channel and the body can. The decision's own consequence, "engineering merges them later", assumed the opposite mistake.
